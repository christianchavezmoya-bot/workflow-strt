Strata Workflow App — Sales Presentation
==========================================

OPTION 1 — Recommended launcher
--------------------------------
  Windows: double-click Start-Presentation.bat
  Mac/Linux: double-click Start-Presentation.sh

Your browser opens at http://localhost:8765
Click "Start Presentation" on the opening screen.

OPTION 2 — Open index.html directly
-----------------------------------
Works in Chrome or Edge after unzipping.

OPTION 3 — Blank white screen?
--------------------------------
Use Start-Presentation.bat / .sh instead.
Browsers often block local file apps.

IN THE BROWSER
--------------
Click "Start Presentation" (with voice) or "Start without audio".
Works fully offline after unzip.
