Customers & sites

Create customers in Admin, add physical sites, and understand how they connect to projects and field work.

Step 1

Overview — why customers and sites matter

Every external project is tied to a customer and usually a site (physical location). Customers hold identity (name, industry, logo); sites hold location and contact details used on reports and in the field.

Customer→ Site(s)→ Project→ Assets / Inspections
  • Admin → Customers — manage customer records and navigate to sites.
  • Cards view — one card per customer; flip to edit; View Sites opens the sites page.
  • Table view — site-centric rows; useful when you manage many locations across customers.
Sites are optional on a project (No site), but linking a site auto-fills location context on reports and helps field teams find the right address.

Step 3

Create a customer

Try it mode: click New Client to create a demo customer.

  1. In Cards view, click New Client.
  2. A new customer card appears with an auto-generated ID (format CUST-…).
  3. The card flips to edit mode — enter the customer name and industry.
  4. Click Save to persist.

Step 4

Edit customer details & upload logo

  1. On a customer card, use the edit control to flip the card.
  2. Update Customer Name and Industry as needed.
  3. Click the logo area to open Upload Customer Logo.
  4. Choose shape (No Shape, Round, Rectangular) and adjust size sliders.
  5. Upload an image and click Save.

Step 5

Add sites for a customer

Try it mode: add a site on the dedicated sites page.

  1. From a customer card, click View Sites (N) — opens /admin/customers/:id/sites.
  2. Page title: Sites - {customer name}; subtitle explains physical locations.
  3. Click Add New Site.
  4. Fill Site Name, City, State, Country, and contact fields.
  5. Click Save on the site card.

Alternative: in Admin Table view, use New Site to add a site row and pick the customer from the dropdown.

Step 6

How customers & sites link to projects

When you create a project (see Projects guide), the Customer dropdown lists all customers. Selecting a customer auto-fills Customer ID and filters the Site dropdown to that customer's locations.

  • Site format in the project form: {Customer} - {Site Name}
  • Choosing a site can auto-switch the customer if you picked the site first.
  • If no site exists yet, choose (No site) and add the site in Admin later.
