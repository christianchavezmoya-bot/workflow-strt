# Commtrac Sales Presentation — Narration Script

## Scene 01 — Welcome

**Duration target:** ~14s  
**On-screen title:** Commtrac  
**Subtitle:** Field Operations, Unified

## Scene 02 — The Challenge

**Duration target:** ~15s  
**On-screen title:** The Challenge  
**Subtitle:** Fragmented field work costs time and trust

> Field projects move fast. Paper checklists get lost. Photos sit on personal phones. Issues fall through the cracks. Commtrac brings every project, asset, and action into one trusted system.

## Scene 03 — One Platform

**Duration target:** ~14s  
**On-screen title:** One Platform, Every Device  
**Subtitle:** Web, Android, and iOS

> One application. Three ways to work. Desktop for planners and managers. The same experience on Android and iOS for technicians in the field. Everyone stays aligned.

**Duration target:** ~14s  
**On-screen title:** Projects  
**Subtitle:** Your command center

> Projects are your command center. Track status, contacts, inspections, and documents in one place. See what's on schedule—and what needs attention—at a glance.

## Scene 05 — Assets & Field Work

**Duration target:** ~15s  
**On-screen title:** Assets  
**Subtitle:** Where field work happens

> The Assets workspace is where field work happens. Select a project, find your equipment, and launch the right workflow. Status, history, and documents travel with every asset.

## Scene 06 — Guided Workflows

**Duration target:** ~15s  
**On-screen title:** Guided Workflows  
**Subtitle:** Step-by-step excellence

## Scene 07 — Issues & Visibility

**Duration target:** ~14s  
**On-screen title:** Issues & Dashboard

## Scene 08 — Signatures & Reports

**Duration target:** ~14s  
**On-screen title:** Signatures & Reports

## Scene 09 — Documents & Knowledge

## Scene 10 — Offline Mobile

**Duration target:** ~15s
