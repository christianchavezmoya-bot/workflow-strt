Installer Field Guide

How to find your jobs, open assets, take over work when needed, and complete a workflow from start to sign-off.

Strata N-Go · Field operations · For Installer role

1. Sign in

Open the app in your browser or on your phone. Enter the email and password your project manager gave you, then tap Sign in.

After first login you may see a welcome tour — tap Skip for now to go straight to your dashboard.

2. Your home base — the Dashboard

Installers land on the Dashboard (/). Your main tab is My Installs — this shows work assigned to you, not the whole company.

My Jobs Today

This is your daily work list — assets assigned to you, sorted by recent activity. Each card shows:

  • Asset tag and job number
  • Status — e.g. Not Started, In Progress, Paused, Pending sign
  • Progress — step completion percentage
  • A primary action button — Start Run, Continue Run, Resume Run, Add Missing Photos, or Installer Sign-off
Tip: Tap +N more — view all at the bottom of My Jobs Today to open the full Project Assets page filtered to your work.

3. Project Assets page

Open Assets in the sidebar (route: /installations/assets). Page title: Project Assets.

  • Pick a project from the job dropdown (or All projects)
  • On phone: toggle My Assets to see only your assignments
  • Use Status filters — Not Started, In Progress, Paused, Pending, Complete, etc.
  • Search by asset tag, serial number, or installer name

The Assigned Tech column shows who owns the job. Your name should appear on assets assigned to you.

4. Taking over someone else's asset

There is no separate “Take over” menu. You take over work by starting or continuing a workflow on an asset that is unassigned or assigned to another person.

  1. Find the asset on the Dashboard or Project Assets page.
  2. Tap Start Run, Continue Run, or Resume Run.
  3. A confirmation dialog appears:
    • Unassigned asset — the app assigns it to you and notifies the project manager.
    • Asset assigned to someone else — you confirm reassignment to yourself.
  4. Tap Assign to me & Start (Assets page) or Continue (Dashboard).
  5. The workflow runner opens — you can begin field work.
Important: You can only assign an asset to yourself. You cannot assign work to a colleague — that is done by a Project Manager or Admin.

5. Workflow journey — start to sign-off

Every installation or inspection follows the same high-level path:

Setup→ Run steps→ Summary & lock→ Installer sign→ Customer sign

Step A — Setup

After you start a run, the runner shows Run workflow with the asset ID, workflow name, and step count. Tap Start → (or Continue → if resuming).

Step B — Complete each workflow step

Work through steps one at a time. For each step you may need to:

  • Fill in text, numbers, or choices
  • Take required photos (marked mandatory — you cannot finish without them)
  • Select your name from Installed By / team dropdowns
  • View reference photos or videos attached by the office (tap thumbnails in Content)

Use Next step → to advance. On the last step, tap Complete. You can Pause anytime and Resume Run later from the dashboard.

Step C — Summary and lock the run

When all steps are done, you see Workflow complete. Review:

  • Steps completed count
  • Any missing photos or blocking issues
  • Recorded time (use Adjust time if needed)

Fix blockers with Add Missing Photos or Resolve Blocking Issue(s). When everything is clear, tap Lock run.

Locking saves the run permanently — after you sign, you cannot edit captured data (PM/Admin can until customer sign-off).

Step D — Installer sign-off

After locking (or from Installer Sign-off on the dashboard), complete Field sign-off:

  • Your name (required)
  • Outcome — Completed, or Conditional
  • Signature (optional draw)
  • Notes (optional)
  • Check the confirmation box that time and captured data are correct

Tap Sign & continue. The run moves to customer sign-off (often collected on site or via email link from the PM).
